A few useful answers

Before we
get started.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the content, the design, and any features you need. We’ll talk through those details and send a proposal with an agreed scope and costs.

How long will it take?

We’ll set a timeline once we understand the project. The scope, availability of content, and review rounds all affect the schedule.

Can you improve our existing website?

Yes. We can review your current website and recommend what to keep, rewrite, or redesign. If it uses a particular platform, we’ll check what is practical before agreeing to the work.

Do we need all the copy and photos ready?

You can start with what you have. We’ll identify the content needed, help shape the messaging, and agree on any copywriting or image sourcing as part of the proposal.

What happens after launch?

We agree on the handover, hosting arrangements, and any ongoing support before work begins. If you need regular updates or extra features, we can discuss those too.

Can we keep our domain name?

Usually, yes. Share your existing domain and hosting setup during discovery so we can plan the transition with you.

Ready for a website
that feels like you?

Send us your current site, an idea, or simply a little about your business. That’s enough to begin.
