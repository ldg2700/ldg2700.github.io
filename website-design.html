Hosting, domains, third-party subscriptions and ongoing support are agreed separately in the proposal.

A few practical questions.

Can you work with our existing website and domain?

Yes. We start by reviewing the current files, platform and hosting setup, then agree what to keep and what to change.

Can a website include internal business systems?

We can scope web applications and approval or monitoring workflows as a separate part of the project. Access controls, hosting and data handling need to be considered alongside the interface.

How do pricing and timing work?

We prepare the scope around the pages, content, integrations and support needed. Costs and milestones are agreed before the work begins.

What happens after launch?

We agree on handover and any maintenance or updates you need. Ongoing support is not assumed; it is defined in your engagement.
