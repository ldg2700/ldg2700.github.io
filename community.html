Community & industry engagement

Present in business.
Involved beyond it.

From industry conversations to community support, these are some of the people, places and activities that shape Providelabs.

Community / July 2025

Relief visits in Taytay.

The Providelabs team visited Sampalucan 1 and 2 in Barangay San Isidro, Taytay, to share supplies with residents affected by Typhoons Crising, Dante and Emong.

The visit formed part of the company's community activities, with support from its leadership and team.

June 2025

Rice support for residents.

For Father's Day, Providelabs shared an initial 100 bags of rice with residents of San Isidro, Taytay.

June 2024

A gathering with senior citizens.

Providelabs joined a Taytay San Isidro Senior Citizens Association gathering, sharing household items and support. At the gathering, the company also outlined plans to support scholarships for members' dependents.
