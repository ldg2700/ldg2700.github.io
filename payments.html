Before you confirm.

  1. Check your invoice.

    Confirm the service, amount and payment instructions with your Providelabs contact.

  2. Scan or import the QR.

    Open your bank or wallet app. Scan this code from another screen, or import the saved QR image if your app supports that feature.

  3. Verify the recipient.

    The payee should be Providelabs Corporation. Check the displayed amount and any fees before authorizing payment.

  4. Keep your reference.

    Send the transaction reference and invoice details to contact@providelabscorp.com so our team can reconcile the payment.
