A few quick steps.

1

Check your invoice.

Use the amount on your invoice or the amount agreed with our team. If you’re unsure, ask us first.

2

Scan or upload the QR.

Open the QR scanner in your bank or e-wallet app. If you’re on the same phone, save the image and use your app’s gallery or upload option, if available. You can also scan it from another screen.

3

Confirm the details.

Check that the recipient is Providelabs Corporation, enter the agreed amount, and review the transaction details and any fees shown by your app before confirming.

4

Send us the reference.

Keep your receipt. Email your name, invoice or project reference, amount, date, and payment reference so our team can match the payment.

Payment questions

Need a hand?

I’m viewing this on my phone. How do I pay?

Tap “Save QR image.” In your bank or e-wallet app, look for an option to scan from your gallery or upload a QR image. If your app doesn’t support that option, open this page on another device and scan from your phone.

The QR won’t scan. What should I do?

Open the original image at full size or download it again. If your app still cannot read it, contact our team to confirm the available payment options.

My app shows a different recipient.

Pause before confirming the payment and contact Providelabs. The intended recipient for this code is Providelabs Corporation.

I’ve paid but haven’t received a confirmation.

Check the transaction status in your bank or wallet app, then send our team the payment reference and invoice details. Contact us before making another payment for the same invoice.

I’m outside the Philippines. Can I pay?

Contact our team for the payment options available for your project. This page is intended for payments through supported Philippine bank and e-wallet apps.
